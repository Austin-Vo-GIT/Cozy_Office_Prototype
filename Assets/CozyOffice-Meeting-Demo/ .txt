COZY OFFICE - MEETING DEMO
For your existing Unity 6000.4.10f1 project.

INSTALL ON MAC
1. Stop Play mode and save your current scene.
2. Unzip this download in Finder.
3. Drag the CozyOfficeDemo folder from the download's Assets folder into
   the Assets area of Unity's Project window. This is a new folder;
   you do not need to replace your existing Assets folder.
4. Wait for Unity to finish compiling.
5. Open Assets/Scenes/Shaun's Sample Scene.unity.
6. From Unity's TOP menu bar choose Cozy Office > Build Meeting Demo.
7. Press Cmd+S to save the populated scene, then press Play.
8. Click the Game view. WASD moves, E interacts, R resets tasks.

DEMO
Walk to PRINT & PANIC on the left and press E to collect a printout.
Walk to YOUR DESK on the right and press E to submit it.
The objective and messages update on screen.

WHAT THE MENU DOES
Adds an editable office with a floor, two open walls, windows, two desks,
chairs, laptops, a printer station, coffee counter and vending machine.
Furniture and props reference your existing LowPolyOfficeInterior prefabs.
Floor, walls, window panels and counters use simple colored geometry.
Creates URP material copies for the demo props, preserving source materials.
Keeps your existing character, controller, animator and directional light.
Positions the player and adjusts the existing camera for an elevated view.
Adds a task component with references already assigned.
It only runs in Shaun's Sample Scene and refuses to add a duplicate demo.
It marks the scene as changed; you save it yourself.

GITHUB
Commit the new Assets/CozyOfficeDemo folder, its .meta file, all generated
material .meta files, and the changed Shaun's Sample Scene.unity.
Your co-founder opens the saved scene; they do NOT run the builder again.

VALIDATION / LIMITS
Project scene, scripts, prefab paths and Unity version were inspected.
This environment has no Unity Editor, so compilation, visual layout,
materials, collisions and Play mode still need verification on your Mac.
The material copies cover opaque furniture, not a full pack conversion.
No purchased asset files are bundled in this download.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
If the menu is missing, open Window > General > Console and share the
first red error. If you see pink props, share a Game view screenshot.
To reverse immediately after building, use Edit > Undo before other edits.
The existing ground Plane is retained below the demo floor.
